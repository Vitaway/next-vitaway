# i18n decisions

## Kinyarwanda review (13 Nov 2026)

Remaining English placeholders in `lib/plus/i18n/rw.json` were translated into everyday Rwandan Kinyarwanda, including badge, blood-pressure, care-tab, and home-plan copy.

Medical wording was kept careful: BMI, unit abbreviations (mmHg, mg/dL, mmol/L, SYS, DIA, Kg, lbs), the App Store name, the clinic name, and the emergency number 912 were left as they are. Diabetes is *Diyabete*. Blood pressure uses *umuvuduko wo hejuru / wo hasi* in member-facing text rather than only the English words systolic and diastolic.

**These translations need review by a native Kinyarwanda speaker before they are treated as final**, especially safety copy (very high blood pressure, dizziness, BMI under 18).
